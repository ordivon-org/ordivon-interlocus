# Interlocus Cross-Owner Interfaces

- World ↔ Interlocus: underlying reality vs qualified Interlocus projections/capabilities.
- Runtime ↔ Interlocus: execution/admission/attempt/effect vs capabilities used by execution.
- Computational Possibility ↔ Interlocus: regime-relative computational achievability vs cross-locus availability/realization of capabilities.
- Harness/Host ↔ Interlocus: Agent mediation/work continuity vs Interlocus capability/discovery/binding/forwarding surfaces.
- SCD ↔ Interlocus: computational-description meaning vs Interlocus contract compatibility/refinement/migration.
- Normative ↔ Interlocus: permission/power/jurisdiction/validity vs cross-locus capability/serviceability. `NormativeValidity != InterlocusCapability`.
- Security/Statistics/Probability/Control/Information Theory remain external generic owners/mathematics where applicable.

Historical documents may say `Network` for this owner. In current prose, use `Interlocus` unless referring to the historical name or a conventional networking specialization.
