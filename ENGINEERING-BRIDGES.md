# Interlocus Engineering Bridges

Workstation/network scripts, Cloudflare surfaces, egress anchors, Runtime/Host consumers, Security Range/Defender consumers, shared-storage/remote-service mechanisms, packet/IP/route implementations, discovery/binding systems and observability tooling may consume **Interlocus** research.

Conventional networking implementations are therefore one important engineering realization family, not the definition of the owner.

For finite-Agent consumers, operation-relative relation/binding/currentness projections are an admissible engineering specialization when a real workload benefits from them. They are **not** a mandate to inject the whole Interlocus graph: later consumer experiments show representation benefit can be capacity- and workload-relative and can carry material context/token cost. A projection grants no imported owner authority and must remain source/currentness fenced.

These are engineering evidence/consumers only. No implementation artifact or consumer experiment defines NDF or Interlocus truth backward. In particular, Security×Interlocus J1 remains a negative deletion-essentiality result rather than a production-layer admission.
