## 4. Canonical staged migration protocol

A migration episode may physically combine several changes, but its logical proof roles remain separate.

Let the episode involve any subset of:

- K version/continuity/usage/residual change;
- generation/path change;
- binding/rebinding change;
- claim identity/epoch change.

The canonical reconciliation protocol is:

### M0 — K semantic delta

`DeltaK_Γ(K0,K1,wK)`

defines which contract changes are admitted/meaningful. It does not prove occurrence.

### M1 — H migration occurrence

`η_mig`

records which changes actually occurred, with causal/lineage/time/provenance and bridge witnesses.

It may record one physical episode containing multiple typed changes without collapsing their semantics.

### M2 — Π/J claim-epoch mapping

For every affected claim role:

`MigrateClaim_Γ(ck0,η_mig,wck)=>ck1`.

Unaffected claim roles may retain an epoch only under an explicit preservation witness.

### M3 — J revalidation

Capability/currentness/reachability/serviceability/satisfaction is re-derived under K1, the post-migration claim epochs, current Π/E, H and target time.

No positive standing transports merely because M0/M1/M2 exists.

Canonical laws:

`ContractDelta != MigrationOccurrence`.

`MigrationOccurrence != ClaimEpochTransition`.

`ClaimEpochTransition != StandingTransport`.

`PartialMigrationCompletion != StandingContinuity`.

### Staged Migration Preservation Theorem

For a migration episode that simultaneously changes K version, generation and binding, Whole-NCT remains coherent if:

1. every semantic contract delta is typed by K;
2. H records the realized change/lineage without claiming semantic equivalence;
3. every claim identity affected by version/generation/binding change is migrated or explicitly preserved under claim-owner policy;
4. prior-epoch evidence is retained historically and cannot be spliced into the new epoch without a transport witness;
5. all positive target standings are revalidated after the affected migration roles are complete.

Under these conditions, simultaneous physical change does not require one universal atomic update semantics and cannot create unwitnessed standing continuity.

## 5. Whole-NCT proof DAG

For one target judgement slice:

`S=<claimRole,claimEpoch,targetTime/effectiveCut,targetJ>`

construct a proof-dependency graph `PDG_S`.

Admissible premise strata are:

- `P0`: imported/source facts σ and external-owner receipts;
- `P1`: K syntax/local compatibility/version/requirement witnesses;
- `P2`: independently grounded H occurrences/history available for S;
- `P3`: Π/E grounded from admissible P0–P2 sources under claim-owner policy;

Within one slice, proof edges may progress toward higher strata but may not form a cycle that uses a J conclusion to generate a premise that retroactively certifies that same conclusion.

A J result may authorize/guide a future realization or change only across a strict causal/epoch/effective-scope boundary. The future H occurrence must have its own occurrence/owner evidence when it occurs.

### Same-Slice Proof Acyclicity Theorem

If:

1. P0 facts are externally/source grounded;
2. K-local witnesses do not assert live standing;
3. H occurrence truth is independently grounded rather than inferred solely from the target J;
4. Π/E grounding does not generate source truth/history;
5. J->future-H feedback crosses a strict causal/epoch/effective-scope boundary;
6. no unsupported same-slice fixed-point rule is imported;

then `PDG_S` is acyclic with respect to certification of the target J.

Canonical laws:

`SameSliceJudgementCycle != Witness`.

`FeedbackRequiresTemporalEpochOrExternalGrounding`.

`FutureOccurrenceCannotRetroactivelySelfCertifyItsAuthorizingJudgement`.

`TimeIndexedFeedback != ProofCycle`.

## 13. Final disposition

### INTERFACE REPAIR FREEZE

Why not `CLEAN FREEZE`?

Because independent theory development exposed real interface ambiguities that required explicit repair:

- J rule specialization vs ownership;
- identity/dependency/provenance role typing;
- staged migration semantics;
- same-slice anti-circular proof discipline;

Ignoring these would leave room for invalid implementations or future reasoning even though the underlying NCT carriers were correct.

Why not `NCT REOPEN`?

Because none of the repairs changes the carrier decomposition `<K,Π,H;J>`, owner membrane, Foundation architecture, or any frozen theorem premise. They make interfaces among already-valid components explicit.

Therefore:

- NCT v1 remains frozen unchanged;
- Capability Calculus v1 remains frozen;
- Projection & Currentness Theory v1 remains frozen;
- Operational Realization Theory v1 remains frozen;
- Whole-NCT Interface Reconciliation v1 is frozen alongside them as the canonical cross-axis interpretation contract;
