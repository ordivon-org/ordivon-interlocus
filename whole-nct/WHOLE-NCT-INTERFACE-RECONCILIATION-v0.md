## 2. Local witness vs cross-axis judgement

A recurring ambiguity is resolved by separating local compatibility witnesses from full Network judgements.

### K-local witness

`CompatK_Γ(p,n,w_K)`

means typed clause compatibility/discharge eligibility under K's q/u/χ/θ/version/dependency rules.

It does not establish live capability/currentness.

### Full discharge judgement

`DischargeJ_Γ(K1,K2,Π,H,E,W) => K_residual`

uses K-local compatibility plus any required Π/H/current owner evidence.

Thus the Capability Calculus notation `p ⊣_w n` is reconciled as a K-side witness constructor/eligibility relation, while NCT's full `Discharge_Γ(K1,K2,Π,H)` remains J-level when live/grounded premises matter.

`ClauseCompatibility != LiveDischargeStanding`.

### H-local structure vs J judgement

H owns branch events/cancel/reconciliation history and certificate references. A predicate such as:

`Quiescent_Γ(branch|effectScope,τ,E,H)`

is a J judgement over H plus owner evidence, not an intrinsic event field.

Likewise `RecoveryEstablished`, `RetryAdmissible`, `Independent`, `Reachable` and `Serviceable` are J-level derived relations even when their rule was developed in the H or Π programme.

## 5. Proof-dependency stratification / anti-circularity

Whole-NCT allows feedback across time/epochs but forbids same-slice self-certification.

For one judgement target `(claim,epoch,τ)`, a proof may depend on:

1. imported/source facts `σ` and external-owner receipts;
2. K syntax/local compatibility witnesses;
3. already grounded H occurrences/history preceding or otherwise independently established for the target;
4. Π projections/evidence grounded in 1–3;
5. J inference over those premises.

A J result may constrain/authorize a **future** H event, K transition or projection target, but that future object cannot be used retroactively to justify the same J result without an external well-founded/fixed-point witness.

Canonical laws:

`SameSliceJudgementCycle != Witness`.

`FeedbackRequiresTemporalEpochOrExternalGrounding`.

`JudgementMayGuideFutureRealizationWithoutSelfCertifyingCurrentStanding`.

This is the cross-axis extension of `ContractCycle != CapabilityWitness` and `NoUnwitnessedCapabilityCreation`.

## 8. Projection refinement vs H abstraction

Projection conservative refinement and H hiding do not commute unconditionally.

Counterexample shape:

1. Π1 hides an internal dependency because targetJ does not currently observe it;
2. H2 composition introduces a future continuation/independence judgement requiring that dependency;
3. applying H composition after Π refinement differs from first expanding the target interface and then refining.

Therefore commutation is admitted only for fixed `Ω_NCT`, target J, time and epoch, with all newly composed continuation obligations already included.

`ProjectionRefinementDoesNotCommuteWithHistoryCompositionByDefault`.

`CrossAxisCommutationRequiresFixedInterfaceContract`.

## 9. End-to-end Serviceability proof shape

A non-circular Network serviceability derivation has the shape:

1. `K.Require(d)` states what must be satisfied;
2. Π discovery may establish `ClaimMatch(candidate)` only;
3. H resolution/binding occurrence establishes a grounded binding lineage when it actually occurs;
4. Π/E plus claim-owner policy may establish `VerifiedCapability` for the correct identity/epoch;
5. J `Reachable` requires current binding/path/support evidence and relevant H;
6. J `Serviceable(candidate,K,τ,E,Π,H)` requires the contract-relevant combination of capability, reachability, resource/continuity/time/currentness and owner bridge predicates;
7. a later realized outcome is evaluated by `Eval` for satisfaction/residual.

Non-implications remain:

`ClaimMatch ⇏ VerifiedCapability ⇏ Reachable ⇏ Serviceable ⇏ RuntimeAttemptSucceeded ⇏ ExternalEffectTrue`.

`ServiceabilityIsDerivedNotSelfGrounding`.

## 10. End-to-end recovery proof shape

1. H records failure/degrade history;
2. H may record repair/failover/recovery occurrences;
3. Π/E obtains fresh evidence in the post-recovery claim epoch;
4. J revalidates identity/currentness/health/binding/reachability;
5. K continuity/version/deadline/usage requirements are checked;
6. J derives recovered serviceability/satisfaction standing if all required premises hold;
7. historical degraded interval remains intact.

`RecoveryOccurrence != RecoveredStanding`.

`RecoveredStandingRequiresKPiHJReconciliation`.

## 11. End-to-end retry proof shape

1. prior H/bridge history identifies the earlier request/attempt/occurrence lineage;
2. Π/E imports the owner-witnessed effect regime: KNOWN_NO_EFFECT / KNOWN_EFFECT / UNKNOWN_EFFECT;
3. K is checked for current retry goal, time, usage, identity and duplicate-effect constraints;
4. J derives `RetryAdmissible` only with required effect-scope witness;
5. if acted upon, the retry becomes a **new future H occurrence/bridge event** with explicit lineage;
6. later outcome/evidence is evaluated anew.

The future retry event cannot be used to prove the prior `RetryAdmissible` judgement that authorized it.

`RetryDecisionPrecedesRetryOccurrenceInProofDependency`.
